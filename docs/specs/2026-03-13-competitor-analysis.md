### Key Takeaways for Your App

1. **Valuation-focused competitors are few.** Only Simply Wall St, GuruFocus, Finbox, and Alpha Spread offer built-in intrinsic value models. Most platforms just display ratios.

2. **AI is still nascent.** Only TipRanks (Spark), GuruFocus (GuruAI), Seeking Alpha (Quant/Virtual Analyst), and Macroaxis have meaningful AI features. There is a wide-open opportunity for AI-powered valuation analysis.

3. **Your pricing sweet spot is $10-30/mo.** Most consumer-grade tools cluster here. Below $10/mo you compete with free tools; above $30/mo you're in prosumer territory.

4. **Your 11-model approach is unique.** No competitor offers 11 different valuation models with company-type classification. This is a genuine differentiator.

5. **Claude AI integration is a major advantage.** Only GuruFocus and TipRanks have comparable AI analysis, both at much higher price points ($449/yr and $360/yr respectively).

6. **Data freshness matters.** Users expect at minimum daily fundamental updates and real-time prices. Your Supabase + FMP refresh pipeline handles this well.

7. **The biggest gap in the market** is an affordable ($10-25/mo) platform that combines multiple valuation models + AI-generated investment theses + clean modern UI. That is exactly what your app does.
